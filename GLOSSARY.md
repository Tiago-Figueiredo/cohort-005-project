# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get back exactly the amount they paid for a Purchase. Once Approved, the learner's Access to the course is Revoked; the Purchase itself still happened and their Progress is kept.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, partial refund

**Requested** / **Approved** / **Declined**:
The three states of a Refund. A Refund is Requested until a Cadence admin Approves or Declines it.
_Avoid_: Pending, open, rejected, accepted

**Resolved**:
A Refund that has been Approved or Declined. A Resolved Refund is final.
_Avoid_: Finalized, closed, completed

**Resolution Note**:
The admin's written reason for Resolving a Refund. Required to Decline, and to Approve despite a Refund Warning.
_Avoid_: Admin comment, decline reason

**Refund Window**:
The fixed period after a Purchase during which the learner may request a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cooling-off period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a learner may currently request a Refund for a Purchase.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refund Warning**:
A condition the admin sees when reviewing a Refund, such as heavy watching or high completion, that does not stop the learner from requesting it.
_Avoid_: Soft limit, soft wall

**Chargeback**:
Reserved for a dispute the cardholder raises with their bank, outside Cadence. It is never another name for a Refund.

### Learning

**Access**:
A learner's right to open a course's lessons. It comes from a Purchase and ends when a Refund is Approved.
_Avoid_: Entitlement, enrollment (as a synonym)

**Revoked**:
Access that has been ended by an Approved Refund. Buying the course again restores it.
_Avoid_: Unenrolled, removed, deleted

**Progress**:
The learner's record of which lessons they have worked through. It belongs to the learner, not the Purchase, and survives a Refund.
_Avoid_: Completion data, course state
